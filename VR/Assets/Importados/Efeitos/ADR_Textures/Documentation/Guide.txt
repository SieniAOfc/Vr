===================================================================
                  RAIN PARTICLE EFFECTS (URP) - USER GUIDE
===================================================================

Welcome! This guide will walk you through setting up and customizing
the 5 rain particle systems in your Unity project.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GETTING STARTED (STEP-BY-STEP)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Check Pipeline
  - Make sure your project is using the Universal Render Pipeline (URP).

Step 2: Locate the Prefabs
  - Navigate to: Assets/ADR_Textures/Content/Prefabs/URP/

Step 3: Add Rain to Your Scene
  - Drag your desired rain prefab into your Scene or Hierarchy.
  - Position the particle system above your main camera or level area.
  - Pro Tip: Parent the particle system to your Main Camera if you want 
    the rain to follow the player across large maps!

-------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PREFAB BREAKDOWN
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PFX_Rain_Drizzle   : Gentle, light rainfall for calm scenes.
2. PFX_Rain_Mist      : Soft atmospheric moisture and haze.
3. PFX_Rain_HeavyMist : High-density fog/mist for swamps or storms.
4. PFX_Rain_Downpour  : Heavy vertical rainfall for intense weather.
5. PFX_Rain_StormWind : High-speed wind-blown rain for violent storms.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW TO CUSTOMIZE THE EFFECTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Select any rain particle prefab in your Hierarchy to adjust settings:

- Rain Amount (Density):
  -> Open 'Emission' module -> Adjust 'Rate over Time'.

- Rain Speed:
  -> Open 'Main' module -> Adjust 'Start Speed'.

- Wind Angle & Direction:
  -> Rotate the GameObject on the X/Z axes, or adjust values inside 
     the 'Force over Lifetime' module.

- Coverage Area:
  -> Open 'Shape' module -> Scale the box/emitter dimensions to match 
     your environment size.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
4. TROUBLESHOOTING
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Why are my particles showing up bright pink?
A: Ensure your project has the Universal Render Pipeline (URP) package 
   installed and active in Project Settings > Graphics.
